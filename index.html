Value of Computation · adaptive reasoning

How much thought is worth spending?

A lightweight simulation of Rational Metareasoning. Instead of always reasoning step-by-step, the model asks whether the expected gain from extra computation is worth the token cost.

Drag the charms below. Adjust utility and cost to watch the reasoning decision change.

Method

From question to reasoning policy

The repository computes two answer likelihoods, prices the extra reasoning tokens, and uses their difference to decide whether to spend more computation.

01

Question

Read a task and its gold answer.

02

Direct likelihood

Score the answer without an added reasoning trace.

03

CoT likelihood

Score the same answer after chain-of-thought.

04

Price thought

Multiply reasoning-token count by γ.

05

Choose

Positive VOC uses CoT; otherwise answer directly.
